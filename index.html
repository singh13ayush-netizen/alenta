About Alenta

A science-led healthcare
company, built to last.

Alenta Lifesciences Private Limited develops, manufactures, and delivers pharmaceutical and healthcare solutions built on scientific evidence and rigorous quality systems. Across every stage—from development and manufacturing to distribution—we uphold the same standards of precision, compliance, and reliability. Together with healthcare professionals, regulators, and partners, we deliver trusted medicines and healthcare solutions that make a lasting difference, one batch at a time. Advancing Together.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What does Alenta Lifesciences do?+

We develop, manufacture and distribute healthcare solutions across pharmaceutical formulations, nutraceuticals, diagnostics, and contract manufacturing — all under one quality standard.

What does "third-party tested" actually mean here?+

Every production batch is sent to an independent lab and assayed for potency, purity, and contaminants before it's approved for release — not just occasionally spot-checked.

Do you work with distribution or contract manufacturing partners?+

Yes — our Contract Manufacturing division supports partners from formulation through scale-up. Reach out through our contact form to start a conversation.

Where are your facilities located?+

We partner with leading pharmaceutical manufacturers pan India, combining trusted expertise with reliable manufacturing capabilities to deliver high-quality healthcare solutions.

How can I apply for a role at Alenta?+

We're always open to hearing from people who share our standard. Send your details through our contact form and our team will be in touch.
